# Jira: Ceremonies

In agile teams, **ceremonies** are the recurring meetings that structure the work. They are often called Scrum events, and they give the team regular points to plan, synchronize, inspect the work, and improve the process. Jira supports these meetings through its boards, backlog, sprint, and reports, which provide the information each ceremony needs.

## Backlog Refinement

Refinement is an ongoing activity, not always a formal meeting. The team reviews upcoming items in the backlog, clarifies their descriptions, estimates their size, and removes items that are no longer needed. The goal is to keep the top of the backlog ready, so planning does not stall on unclear work.

## Sprint Planning

Sprint planning happens at the start of each sprint. The team agrees on a sprint goal, pulls issues from the top of the backlog, and confirms that the chosen work fits the capacity of the team. Each issue should be clear enough that the team can start it without waiting for more information.

Planning answers two questions: what can we deliver this sprint, and how will we do it?

## Daily Standup

The daily standup is a short meeting, usually limited to about fifteen minutes, where each team member shares progress, plans for the day, and any blockers. It is a synchronization point, not a status report to a manager. On a Jira board, the standup often starts by looking at the sprint board together, so the conversation focuses on issues that are moving, stuck, or waiting.

Blockers raised in standup should lead to action after the meeting, not long discussions during it.

## Sprint Review

At the end of the sprint, the team demonstrates the work it completed to stakeholders and gathers feedback. The review focuses on the product: what works, what changed, and what should happen next. Issues that were not finished are discussed, and the backlog is updated with feedback that came out of the review.

## Sprint Retrospective

The retrospective happens after the review and is for the team alone. The team looks at how it worked during the sprint, identifies what went well, what did not, and what to change. It usually produces a small number of concrete improvement actions, which the team tracks as issues in the next sprint.

Jira's sprint reports, such as the velocity chart and the burndown chart, give the retrospective useful data to discuss.

## Key Idea

Each ceremony has one job. Refinement keeps the backlog ready, planning commits to a goal, standup keeps the team in sync, review shows the work, and retrospective improves the way the team works. When each meeting stays focused on its purpose, the sprint runs smoothly.
